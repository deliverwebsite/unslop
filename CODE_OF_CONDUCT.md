# Code of conduct

unslop is a friendly project. Everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else
connected to it) agrees to the following.

## Expected

- Be kind and assume good intent. Critique ideas, not people.
- Give specific, useful feedback, and accept it gracefully.
- Make room for newcomers and people with less experience.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes or language.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing a conversation.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Spam or self-promotion unrelated to the discussion.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues or pull requests, and block people who break these rules.
To report a problem privately, email hello@deliverwebsite.com. Reports are kept
confidential.
